# Riding in winter

!!! WARNING "Under Development"

Riding in winter can be enjoyable with a little extra planning.

- choose safer routes, avoiding sharp corners and steep descents
- main roads are likely to have less debris, as previous traffic clears the roads
- wear breathable layers to keep warm without getting wet
- drink plenty of water before, during and after the ride
- warm-up before starting the ride
- warm-up before taking a shower to avoid chillblains (perniosis)

## Cold & Wet Weather Kit

- Leg warmers or bib-tights
- Arm warmers
- Gilet
- Thicker jacket
- Mid-layer fleece
- Thicker base layer
- Warmer Gloves and glove liners
- Over-socks
- Over-shoes
- Snood (neck warmer)
