## Tools

Most tools and services are semi-commercial, with limits on free use without a paid-for subscription.

- [Climb Finder](https://climbfinder.com/) - satistics and visuals for a large collection of hills.
- [BikeClimbs](https://bikeclimbs.com/) - top bike climbs by country (has links for VeloViewer, Google Street View and Strava)
- [Interactive Hill Climb Calculator](https://www.broleur.com/hill-climb-calculator/) - calculate hill climb time based on assumed effort

[Climb Finder](https://climbfinder.com/en/climbs/toy-s-hill-four-elms){target=_blank} to search for climbs by region to help planning a route. Providing statistics to support improvement of a riders climbing capability.

[Velo Viewer](https://veloviewer.com/){target=_blank} is a commercial service providing a level of climb details to professional cyclist level, for those looking to optimise their training.


### Ride With GPS

Each routes show an elevation cross section panel which can optionally show a color gradient.

Selecting a section of the route, e.g. a specific hill, on the elevation shows the gradient in more detail for that section of the route.
